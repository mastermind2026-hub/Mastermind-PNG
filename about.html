About MASTERMIND

MASTERMIND is an educational digital publishing platform dedicated to empowering Papua New Guinean entrepreneurs, small business owners, and digital creators.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the digital gap in Papua New Guinea by providing free, high-quality, practical guides on social media marketing, digital commerce, product sourcing, and peer-to-peer cryptocurrency fundamentals.

What We Provide

  • Actionable Marketing Strategies: Step-by-step guides tailored to local customer acquisition via Facebook and WhatsApp.
  • Safe Trade Frameworks: Educational insights into digital asset security and peer-to-peer verification.
  • Sourcing & Logistics Knowledge: Practical guidance on importing goods, calculating landed costs, and finding reliable international suppliers.
